# Third-party sources and licensing

The ADAMSLab feeder implementation is redistributed with its original MIT license in `licenses/ADAMSLab_LICENSE.txt`. Its source commit and acquisition manifests are retained. That license applies to the upstream material it covers; it is not asserted to license every file in this repository.

Source metadata and derived research inputs refer to IEEE test feeders and the published CLPU study. Third-party journal PDFs are excluded. No new blanket license has been selected for author-created code/data by the packaging process. The authors may add an appropriate license before public release; until then, public access alone should not be interpreted as a blanket reuse grant.
